# KAIU

Natural luxury wood veneer — crafted for architects, interior designers, and furniture makers who value authentic grain and refined, modern craftsmanship.

### About

KAIU sources and finishes premium wood veneer across six collections — Athens, Berlin, Cairo, Cannes, Oslo, and Zurich — each with its own character, from defined natural lines to quiet, precision-cut minimalism. We work with studios and homeowners on custom panelling, furniture, and architectural surfaces.
